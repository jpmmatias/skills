Example course

Probability & Statistics, by experiment

Three lessons for a developer who runs A/B tests and reads dashboards: simulate randomness, measure how much a metric should wobble, and weigh a “winning” result against how often wins are real.

This is a shipped example of a Learncraft course. It has no learner: nothing has been practised, no milestone has evidence, and the mission in MISSION.md describes a typical reader, not a person. Practice you do here is saved only in your own browser.
